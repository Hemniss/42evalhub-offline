Preliminaries

Submission

  • As usual, clone the Git repository of the learner you are evaluating.
    It must contain a file with a link to the video hosted on a platform
    such as YouTube, Dailymotion, etc.

  • This evaluation can be completed without the presence of the person
    being evaluated. However, if this is abused, mandatory attendance
    will be reinstated.

  • If there is no link, the video cannot be viewed and the evaluation
    ends here.

  • Please note that some videos may be "censored" for confidentiality
    reasons. Not everything can be shared in full. Proceed thoughtfully.
    Even if some parts are not explained in detail, the content and
    overall logic must remain understandable.

Submission's style

Technical Aspects

  • The video lasts approximately 5 minutes (±1 minute tolerance).

  • The video includes a presentation made using appropriate software
    (PowerPoint, Prezi, Keynote, MagicPoint, etc.).

  • The visual and audio quality is acceptable (not pixelated, muffled, or inaudible).

Staging and Professionalism

  • The learner's face is visible for most of the video. (It is acceptable
    to include intro/conclusion segments or chapters where the learner is
    in close-up, or temporarily off-screen to display a title.)

  • The overall presentation is professional:
    --> No distracting fonts or flashy backgrounds.
    --> Clean and appropriate clothing; a shirt or suit is recommended.
    --> No inappropriate language or behavior.

  • There is a touch of roleplay: the presentation is addressed to a board,
    another company, or a newly hired colleague.

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Content

Project Presentation and Context

  • You understand the company’s mission, its market, and general context.

  • You understand the learner's role, tasks, and responsibilities,
    especially if part of a team.

  • You understand the purpose of their mission in the company’s context.

  • You understand the outcome: what was achieved, what went well or
    didn’t, and whether the work continued after the learner left.

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Showcasing Skills and Qualities

  • The learner highlights their technical and/or managerial qualities
    without exaggeration.

  • Are their claims (“I can do this,” “I demonstrated that...”)
    supported by concrete examples of work done during the experience?
    This second point should carry significant weight in your evaluation.

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Communication

Oral Communication

  • Does the voice sound calm and composed? (More points)

  • Or is it nervous, hesitant, or overly stressed? (Fewer points)

  • Are gestures deliberate and supportive of the message? Do facial
    expressions and body language convey confidence? Or are there
    distracting nervous tics?

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)
