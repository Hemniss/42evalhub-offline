Introduction

This form allows you to evaluate the learner midway through their
professional experience within your company.
We recommend completing this evaluation in the learner’s presence
whenever possible.

The goal is to assess their progress during the first few months
of their professional experience:
- What went well?
- How well have they integrated into the team?
- What skills were expected, and how has the learner met those
expectations?

This is also an opportunity for the learner to share their impressions:
- Do they feel integrated?
- Are they satisfied with the workload, the technologies used,
the work environment, and the tasks assigned?

Guidelines


This document follows the 42 evaluation structure. You must evaluate
each point based on the learner’s performance.
You should also write a final comment summarizing your evaluation,
which will be visible to the learner.

This evaluation should be completed with the learner whenever possible
and should encourage constructive feedback and discussion.

Each question is graded on a scale from 0 to 5.

Mid-term Evaluation

Technical skills

  • Did the learner's technical skills meet your expectations?
  • Were they consistent with or better than those presented during the recruitment process?

Rating (0 to 5): unacceptable, very insufficient, insufficient,
adequate, good, excellent

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Organization and autonomy

Assess the learner’s ability to stay organized and work
independently:

  • Do they follow your company’s organization(e.g., working hours, dress code)?
  • Are they autonomous and proactive (e.g., seeking documentation or asking colleagues when needed)?

Rating (0 to 5): unacceptable, very insufficient, insufficient,
adequate, good, excellent

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Team integration

  • Has the learner found their place within the team?
  • Do they maintain good relationships with colleagues?

Rating (0 to 5): unacceptable, very insufficient, insufficient,
adequate, good, excellent

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Contribution to the mission

  • Has the learner contributed effectively to the assigned tasks or projects?

Rating (0 to 5): unacceptable, very insufficient, insufficient,
adequate, good, excellent

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Creativity

  • How would you rate the learner’s creativity?

Creativity may be expressed in various ways:
suggesting technical options, offering relevant alternatives,
or proposing unconventional yet effective solutions.

Rating (0 to 5): unacceptable, very insufficient, insufficient,
adequate, good, excellent

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Overall impression

  • What is your overall impression of the learner’s performance during their time in the company?

This question summarizes the previous ones and plays a major role
in determining the final grade for their work experience.

Rating (0 to 5): unacceptable, very insufficient, insufficient,
adequate, good, excellent

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)

Readiness for the professional world

For statistical purposes only (not included in the final grade):

  • To what extent do you believe the learner is ready to enter the professional world?

Rating (0 to 5): 0–19%, 20–39%, 40–59%, 60–79%, 80–99%, 100%

Rate it from 0 (failed) through 5 (excellent)
